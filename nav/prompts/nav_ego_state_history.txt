- Minimap pixel coordinates are provided only to relate the state to the image.
- Yaw relative to the minimap: θ=0°→West, θ=90°→North, θ=180°→East, θ=270°→South.

{history}

## ACTIONS
- `turn right` increases yaw; `turn left` decreases yaw.
- `forward` moves along the current yaw. Infer progress from world X/Z history instead of assuming a fixed pixel step.
- `stop` is valid when world distance is at most {reach_m} m.

## DECISION PRIORITY
1. **USE THE LATEST VIEW FIRST** - In `moving` mode, current visual evidence overrides an older clear or blocked path.
2. **CHECK HISTORY** - Avoid loops and repeated failed actions, but do not assume a moving obstacle remains in the same place.
3. **AVOID OBSTACLES FIRST** - Never choose an action that collides with a static or moving obstacle. Keep clearance because the agent is human-sized.
4. **REDUCE DISTANCE** - Pick a safe allowed action that reduces world distance. Use world X/Z and yaw to decide whether to move or turn.
5. **MAKE PROGRESS** - If distance has not decreased, choose a different safe approach.
6. **STOP** - Stop when world distance is at most {reach_m} m or when no action is currently safe.
